Top 5 Candidates by Intervention

Drag any slider to change how much a factor counts toward that intervention's score. The map follows whichever slider you move, and the menu above the map lets you show any other score. Weights are relative: the tool rescales them so they always add up to 100%, and the percentage beside each slider shows its current share. These weights use standard, widely available GIS layers that relate to, but do not precisely measure, the four thermal comfort drivers in the full report (radiant load, air temperature, humidity/evapotranspiration and wind/ventilation). Ownership is a feasibility factor, not a thermal driver. The tool favors replicability across municipalities over detailed microclimate modeling.
